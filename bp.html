LUJIAN / EYES SEE

AI accessibility and travel support

The project explores how route information, family support and community response might help people with visual impairments feel more confident going out. The current work is an interactive demonstration, with real-world reliability and safety still to be validated.

Competition and development

The early project concept received first place at the 2026 Engineering Girls Future Empowerment Program national final. The associated organizations are KPMG, Yiyou Youth and the China Women’s Development Foundation.

After the competition, I reviewed the original two-interface concept and added a family interface. I rebuilt the product structure around three connected perspectives: the traveler, a family member and community administration. I used AI-assisted development to build the revised interactive demo and updated the business plan.

Three connected interfaces

Traveler: explore route risks and travel information. Family: view a companion-support concept. Administration: explore how reported road risks and facility issues might be reviewed and handled.

My contribution

I reworked the target-user and scenario structure after the competition, defined the three-interface architecture, built the interactive demonstration through AI-assisted development, and revised the business plan around that architecture.

What this demonstrates

The prototype illustrates the proposed interactions and coordination between different users. It does not establish reliable real-time obstacle detection, safe navigation or a deployed public service. The national-final award applies to the early concept, not the later prototype.
